# Product and engineering decisions

Use this file for decisions that affect behavior, records, money, tax, security or deployment. Do not duplicate routine code changes; those belong in `DEVELOPMENT-LOG.md`.

## Confirmed decisions

- Replace Tally for shop operations; the accountant handles GST filing.
- Multi-tenant application, one branch per company for now.
- One normal shop permission level with a separate password/session gate for aggregate profit.
- Signup requires operator approval (`verified: true`) before login.
- One cash drawer and one GPay/bank account.
- Inventory and Purchases remain separate screens in the same navigation group.
- Supplier stock can be received unpaid, sold, and settled later against purchase items.
- Repairs use a separate service invoice; PC-build invoices may contain assembly charges.
- Invoice GST mode can be inclusive or exclusive and intra-state or interstate.
- Retain historical records; do not automatically delete them after one year.
- VPS private storage plus off-server backup is the selected production direction.
- WhatsApp/bulk messaging remains mock for now.
- 2026-09-11 (D-003): Opening setup follows a one-time Draft → Finalized workflow. Draft setup is editable and creates no ledger, stock, or serial records. Finalization executes once in a single MongoDB transaction loading the persisted draft, creating allocatable openingReceivables, openingPayables, opening stock lots, serial units, and opening cash/bank account movements. Once finalized, opening records are permanently read-only; future corrections require current-day audited adjustments in Phase 5.
- 2026-09-11 (D-011): Stock adjustments are idempotent via `idempotencyKey` backed by a partial unique index. Retries return existing movement without duplicating stock or serial records.
- 2026-09-11 (D-012): Changing product `isSerialTracked` is locked whenever physical stock on hand exists for that product.
- 2026-09-12 (D-014): Supplier bill payable liability starts strictly when `billStatus === 'Posted'`. Unposted draft and confirmed purchase orders create zero payable liability and zero stock. Resolves D-005.
- 2026-09-12 (D-015): Supplier bill uniqueness is scoped to `{ tenantId, supplierId, financialYear, supplierInvoiceNumberNormalized }` for posted bills. Different suppliers or different financial years can share identical invoice number strings.
- 2026-09-12 (D-016): Excess payment requires explicit user consent (`recordExcessAsAdvance: true`) and is recorded as an unallocated `supplierAdvances` record. Over-allocation without consent is rejected. Resolves D-004.
- 2026-09-12 (D-017): Single authoritative credit engine: all spendable and refundable supplier credits reside exclusively in `supplierAdvances`. Accepted credit notes credit the specific returned bill line; any excess creates an advance. Return on Line A never reduces Line B due.
- 2026-09-12 (D-018): Payment reversal is blocked with HTTP 400 if the advance generated by that payment was partly or fully consumed by downstream allocations or refunds.
- 2026-09-12 (D-019): Cash overdraft is strictly blocked; disbursements require `{ balancePaise: { $gte: amountPaise } }`.
- 2026-09-12 (D-020): Bank overdraft is strictly blocked in Phase 3; disbursements require `{ balancePaise: { $gte: amountPaise } }`.
- 2026-09-12 (D-021): Operational posting dates must be the current open business day in Asia/Kolkata (`todayInKolkata()`).
- 2026-09-12 (D-022): Operational posting dates must be strictly after the finalized opening cutoff date (`> cutoffDate`). Records on or before cutoff date are rejected.
- Excluded scope is listed in `ANTIGRAVITY-DEVELOPMENT-PROMPT.md` and `DEVELOPMENT-PHASES.md`.
