# Implementation Plan — Phase 3: Purchases, Stock Receipt, and Supplier Settlement (Definitive Architecture)

This document establishes the complete, production-grade architecture, data schemas, database indexes, state machines, financial reconciliation logic, concurrency guards, API contracts, UI integration mappings, and acceptance test matrix for **Phase 3: Purchases, Stock Receipt, and Supplier Settlement**.

---

## Approved Policy Decisions & Operational Invariants

- **D-014 (Bill Payable Timing):** A supplier payable liability is created strictly when a bill is posted (`billStatus: 'Posted'`). An unbilled Purchase Order tracks commercial commitment only and generates **zero** payable liability and **zero** entry in supplier dues.
- **D-015 (Supplier Bill Uniqueness):** Scoped to `{ tenantId, supplierId, financialYear, supplierInvoiceNumberNormalized }` where `billStatus: 'Posted'`. Blank draft numbers do not conflict; identical bill numbers from different suppliers do not conflict. `financialYear` is derived from `supplierInvoiceDate` (April 1 to March 31).
- **D-016 (Excess Payment / Advance):** Any payment amount exceeding selected bill dues requires an explicit user action/toggle: `"Record excess as supplier advance"`. Excess is recorded in `supplierAdvances`.
- **D-017 (Return Credit Allocation):** Accepted supplier credit notes on paid lines are recorded as available credit in `supplierAdvances` and must be **explicitly allocated** by the operator to other bills or refunded. A return on paid Line A never silently reduces unpaid Line B due.
- **D-018 (Payment Reversal Dependencies):** Reversing a payment is blocked if any portion of an advance generated by it was allocated to other bills or refunded. Downstream allocations must be reversed first.
- **D-019 (Cash Overdraft Policy):** **Strictly blocked.** Cash balance cannot become negative (`balancePaise >= 0`). Any outgoing movement exceeding available cash aborts the transaction.
- **D-020 (Bank Overdraft Policy):** **Strictly blocked in Phase 3.** Bank balance cannot become negative (`balancePaise >= 0`). No override setting (`allowBankOverdraft`) is permitted in Phase 3. A controlled company setting may be introduced later through an audited company setting if a real overdraft facility exists.
- **D-021 (Posting Period & Cutoff Boundary):** All operational posting events (receipts, payments, credits, advances, returns, refunds, reversals) use the **current open business date in Asia/Kolkata** (`todayInKolkata()`). `supplierInvoiceDate` is preserved separately as the printed date on the supplier's bill.
- **Opening Cutoff Boundary:** Opening balances represent the company position at the end of `cutoffDate`. Operational posting dates must be **strictly later than `cutoffDate`** ($\text{postingDate} > \text{cutoffDate}$). If the current Kolkata date $\le \text{cutoffDate}$, operational posting is blocked with a setup configuration error (`HTTP 400: Operational posting date must be after opening cutoff date`).

## 2. Separation of Purchase Lifecycle States

To prevent state collisions and ambiguous document conditions, the single overloaded `status` field is replaced with four independent orthogonal projections:

```typescript
documentStatus: 'Draft' | 'Confirmed' | 'Cancelled';
billStatus:     'NotPosted' | 'Posted' | 'Credited' | 'FullyCredited';
receiptStatus:  'NotReceived' | 'PartlyReceived' | 'Received' | 'ClosedPartlyReceived';
paymentStatus:  'NotApplicable' | 'Unpaid' | 'PartlyPaid' | 'Paid';
```

### Orthogonal Definitions
1. **`documentStatus`:** Tracks the lifecycle of the purchase document itself:
   - `'Draft'`: Editable document preparation. No commercial commitment, no inventory, no liability.
   - `'Confirmed'`: Confirmed commercial order or finalized purchase. Rates and supplier identity locked.
   - `'Cancelled'`: Unbilled, unreceived order cancelled with zero side effects.
2. **`billStatus`:** Tracks the commercial supplier bill and financial liability:
   - `'NotPosted'`: No supplier bill posted. Generates **zero** payable liability and **zero** entry in supplier dues.
   - `'Posted'`: Supplier bill posted. Rates, HSN, taxes, and supplier invoice details are **permanently immutable**. Generates payable liability.
   - `'Credited'`: At least one credit note or return has credited liability against lines of this bill.
   - `'FullyCredited'`: Total credited liability equals total bill liability. Net due is permanently zero.
3. **`receiptStatus`:** Tracks physical warehouse goods intake:
   - `'NotReceived'`: 0 units received into inventory.
   - `'PartlyReceived'`: $0 < \text{quantityReceived} < \text{quantityOrdered}$.
   - `'Received'`: $\text{quantityReceived} \ge \text{quantityOrdered}$.
   - `'ClosedPartlyReceived'`: Short delivery closed via remainder closure; remaining units cancelled.
4. **`paymentStatus`:** Tracks settlement of the posted liability:
   - `'NotApplicable'`: When `billStatus === 'NotPosted'`. Unposted orders have no payment status.
   - `'Unpaid'`: `billStatus === 'Posted'`, $\text{allocatedPaidPaise} === 0$, $\text{duePaise} > 0$.
   - `'PartlyPaid'`: $\text{allocatedPaidPaise} > 0$ and $\text{duePaise} > 0$.
   - `'Paid'`: $\text{duePaise} === 0$ (settled via payments, credit allocations, or return offsets).

### Valid State Matrix & Transitions
| Scenario / Action | `documentStatus` | `billStatus` | `receiptStatus` | `paymentStatus` | Financial Due? | Stock In Hand? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New Preparation Draft** | `Draft` | `NotPosted` | `NotReceived` | `NotApplicable` | No (0) | No (0) |
| **Confirmed Purchase Order** | `Confirmed` | `NotPosted` | `NotReceived` | `NotApplicable` | No (0) | No (0) |
| **Cancelled Order** | `Cancelled` | `NotPosted` | `NotReceived` | `NotApplicable` | No (0) | No (0) |
| **Bill Posted (Goods Pending)** | `Confirmed` | `Posted` | `NotReceived` | `Unpaid` | Yes (Total) | No (0) |
| **Partial Goods Intake** | `Confirmed` | `Posted` | `PartlyReceived` | `Unpaid` | Yes (Total) | Partial |
| **Partial Payment Made** | `Confirmed` | `Posted` | `PartlyReceived` | `PartlyPaid` | Yes (Remaining) | Partial |
| **Remainder Closed** | `Confirmed` | `Posted` | `ClosedPartlyReceived` | `PartlyPaid` | Yes (Remaining) | Partial (Kept) |
| **Fully Settled & Received** | `Confirmed` | `Posted` | `Received` | `Paid` | No (0) | Full |
| **Bill Fully Offset by Credit** | `Confirmed` | `FullyCredited` | `Received` | `Paid` | No (0) | Full |

### State Query & Filter Rules
- **Dues Query (`hasDue=true`):** Query `{ billStatus: 'Posted', duePaise: { $gt: 0 } }`. Strictly excludes unposted orders.
- **Open Purchase Orders:** Query `{ documentStatus: 'Confirmed', receiptStatus: { $in: ['NotReceived', 'PartlyReceived'] } }`.
- **Awaiting Goods Receipt:** Query `{ billStatus: 'Posted', receiptStatus: { $in: ['NotReceived', 'PartlyReceived'] } }`.

## 3. Editing, Cancellation, and Remainder Rules

1. **Draft Edits:** Allowed with optimistic concurrency locking (`version: number`). If another session modified the draft in the interim, the update fails with HTTP 409 Conflict.
2. **Posted Bill Immutability:** When `billStatus === 'Posted'`, financial fields (`lines.unitCostPaise`, `lines.taxBasisPoints`, `supplierId`, `inclusive`, `taxMode`, `supplierInvoiceNumber`, `supplierInvoiceDate`) are permanently locked. Allowed post-posting updates are restricted to non-financial fields (`notes`, internal tags, file attachments) with full `auditHistory` logging.
3. **Attachment Validation:** `attachmentFileId` must reference a valid file record where `files.tenantId === identity.tenantId`. Injecting an external or cross-tenant file ID is rejected with HTTP 404.
4. **Partial Receipt Remainder Closure (`POST /api/purchases/[id]/close-remainder`):**
   - For an order with 10 units ordered and 6 units received where the remaining 4 units will not be shipped.
   - The line sets `quantityCancelled = quantityOrdered - quantityReceived` (4 units).
   - Document transitions to `receiptStatus: 'ClosedPartlyReceived'`.
   - **Crucial Invariant:** Received stock lots, serial units, and stock movements remain 100% intact.
   - If the supplier billed for all 10 units, closing the remainder does not alter the financial bill total; an audited `supplierCreditNotes` record must be issued to adjust the liability.
5. **Unbilled Order Cancellation (`POST /api/purchases/[id]/cancel`):**
   - Allowed only when `billStatus === 'NotPosted'` and `receiptStatus === 'NotReceived'`.
   - Transitions `documentStatus: 'Cancelled'`. Zero financial or inventory side effects.

## 4. Single Authoritative Available-Credit Engine

To eliminate duplicate representations and prevent double refunds, **all spendable and refundable supplier credit balances reside in exactly one collection: `supplierAdvances`**.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  CN[Supplier Credit Note] -->|allocates directly| BillLine[Purchase Line Due Reduced]
  CN -->|unallocated excess| Adv[SupplierAdvances Record<br>sourceType: CreditNoteExcess]
  Pay[Overpayment / Explicit Advance] --> Adv2[SupplierAdvances Record<br>sourceType: ExplicitAdvance / PaymentOverpay]
  Adv -->|POST /api/purchases/advances/[id]/allocate| LineAlloc[SupplierAllocations Event]
  Adv -->|POST /api/purchases/refunds| Ref[SupplierRefunds Event<br>Posts 'In' to Cash/Bank]
  Adv2 -->|Allocate| LineAlloc
  Adv2 -->|Refund| Ref
```

### Available-Credit Rules
1. **Accepted Credit Notes (`supplierCreditNotes`):** Records the immutable commercial credit granted by the supplier.
2. **Excess Creation:** Any unallocated portion ($\text{unallocatedCreditPaise} = \text{acceptedCreditPaise} - \text{allocatedLiabilityPaise}$) creates **one** `supplierAdvances` record with:
   - `sourceType: 'CreditNoteExcess'`
   - `creditNoteId: string` (traceability back to original credit note)
   - `originalAmountPaise: number`
   - `remainingAmountPaise: number`
   - `allocatedPaise: 0`
   - `refundedPaise: 0`
   - `status: 'Open'`
3. **Refund Target:** `supplierRefunds` records must reference `advanceId` (ref `supplierAdvances._id`). Direct refund from a credit note without going through the authoritative advance balance is **strictly forbidden**.
4. **Advance Balance Invariant:**
   $$\text{remainingAmountPaise} \equiv \text{originalAmountPaise} - \text{allocatedPaise} - \text{refundedPaise}$$
5. **Status Lifecycle:**
   - `status: 'Open'` when $\text{remainingAmountPaise} === \text{originalAmountPaise}$.
   - `status: 'PartlyConsumed'` when $0 < \text{remainingAmountPaise} < \text{originalAmountPaise}$.
   - `status: 'Consumed'` when $\text{remainingAmountPaise} === 0$.
6. **Concurrency Defense:** Any allocation or refund atomically updates `supplierAdvances` with `{ _id: advanceId, remainingAmountPaise: { $gte: requiredAmountPaise } }`. Competing requests that jointly exceed the remaining balance fail cleanly with HTTP 409/400.

## 6. Return Valuation Mathematics & Proration

Returns and credit notes must preserve the original purchase line's net discounts, taxable base, and GST rates. Simple `quantity * unitCostPaise` is strictly prohibited.

### Valuation Equations
For a purchase line with ordered quantity $Q_{\text{ordered}}$, line taxable base $T_{\text{base}}$, line CGST, SGST, IGST, and line total $L_{\text{total}}$:

When returning $Q_{\text{ret}}$ units:
1. **Prorated Taxable Base:**
   $$T_{\text{ret}} = \left\lfloor T_{\text{base}} \times \frac{Q_{\text{ret}}}{Q_{\text{ordered}}} \right\rfloor$$
2. **Prorated Tax Amounts:**
   $$\text{CGST}_{\text{ret}} = \left\lfloor \text{CGST} \times \frac{Q_{\text{ret}}}{Q_{\text{ordered}}} \right\rfloor, \quad \text{SGST}_{\text{ret}} = \left\lfloor \text{SGST} \times \frac{Q_{\text{ret}}}{Q_{\text{ordered}}} \right\rfloor, \quad \text{IGST}_{\text{ret}} = \left\lfloor \text{IGST} \times \frac{Q_{\text{ret}}}{Q_{\text{ordered}}} \right\rfloor$$
3. **Total Credit Value:**
   $$C_{\text{ret}} = T_{\text{ret}} + \text{CGST}_{\text{ret}} + \text{SGST}_{\text{ret}} + \text{IGST}_{\text{ret}}$$
4. **Final Partial Return Rounding Reconciliation:**
   When the final remaining units of a line are returned ($Q_{\text{ret}} + Q_{\text{previouslyReturned}} === Q_{\text{ordered}}$), the return calculation allocates any rounding remainder so that:
   $$\sum C_{\text{ret}} \equiv L_{\text{total}}$$
   The calculated `roundingRemainderPaise` is recorded on the line for audit verification.

### Financial-Only Credit Notes (Price / Freight Rebates)
- Do not affect physical inventory lots or serial units (0 stock movement).
- Explicit credit lines specify description, `taxableBasePaise`, `taxBasisPoints`, and tax breakdown.

## 7. Reversal Workflows & Policy

### 1. Direct Payment Allocation Reversal
- **Problem:** Reversing a direct payment allocation cannot restore funds to a `supplierPaymentComponent` because components are immutable disbursement records without mutable balances.
- **Adopted Policy:**
  1. The target line's `remainingDuePaise` is increased by `allocPaise` (due restored).
  2. Creates an unallocated `supplierAdvances` record:
     - `sourceType: 'ReversalCredit'`
     - `paymentId: originalPaymentId`
     - `reversalAllocationId: newReversalAllocationDoc._id`
     - `originalAmountPaise: allocPaise`
     - `remainingAmountPaise: allocPaise`
     - `status: 'Open'`
  3. Cash/Bank balance is **not** touched (the supplier still holds the disbursed funds).
  4. Appends an immutable reversal record in `supplierAllocations` (`isReversal: true, reversesAllocationId: originalId`).
  5. The created advance can then be allocated to another bill or refunded via `supplierRefunds`.

### 2. Advance or Credit Allocation Reversal
- Target line `remainingDuePaise` is increased by `allocPaise`.
- Source `supplierAdvances`: atomically `$inc: { remainingAmountPaise: allocPaise, allocatedPaise: -allocPaise }`.
- Cash/Bank does not move.
- Appends offsetting `supplierAllocations` record.

### 3. Full Payment Reversal (`POST /api/purchases/payments/[id]/reverse`)
- **Preconditions:**
  1. All direct allocations of the payment must have been reversed.
  2. Any `supplierAdvances` generated from the payment must have $\text{remainingAmountPaise} === \text{originalAmountPaise}$ ($\text{allocatedPaise} === 0$ and $\text{refundedPaise} === 0$).
  3. If any downstream allocation or refund depends on the advance, the reversal is **strictly blocked** with HTTP 400: `"Cannot reverse payment: advance ADV-... has been partly or fully consumed. Unwind downstream allocations/refunds first."`
- **Execution:**
  1. Closes the advance (`status: 'Consumed', remainingAmountPaise: 0`).
  2. Sets `supplierPayments.isReversed = true`.
  3. Atomically increments `tenantAccountBalances.balancePaise` for each payment component.
  4. Appends incoming `accountMovements` (`qty: +compAmountPaise`, reason: `'Payment reversal'`).

### 4. Credit Note Reversal (`POST /api/purchases/credit-notes/[id]/reverse`)
- **Preconditions:** Any advance generated by this credit note must have $\text{remainingAmountPaise} === \text{originalAmountPaise}$. All direct bill allocations must be reversed first.
- **Execution:** Closes the advance; marks `supplierCreditNotes.isReversed = true`; inserts `supplierCreditNoteReversals` record. Statement sign: Positive (`+ acceptedCreditPaise`), restoring shop liability.

### 5. Refund Reversal (`POST /api/purchases/refunds/[id]/reverse`)
- **Preconditions:** The shop must have sufficient funds in Cash/Bank to repay the reversed refund.
- **Execution:** Atomically decrements `tenantAccountBalances.balancePaise`; appends outgoing `accountMovements` (`qty: -amountPaise`); restores source `supplierAdvances` balance (`$inc: { remainingAmountPaise: amountPaise, refundedPaise: -amountPaise }`); marks `supplierRefunds.isReversed = true`; inserts `supplierRefundReversals` record. Statement sign: Negative (`- amountPaise`), restoring available credit.

## 8. Signed Supplier Statement Mathematics

### Sign Convention
- **Positive (+):** Shop owes supplier (Liability).
- **Negative (-):** Supplier owes shop (Asset / Credit).

### Complete Sign Table by Event Type
| Event Type | Sign in Statement | Math Formula Impact | Account Movement? | Net Position Delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 2 Opening Payable** | **+ (Positive)** | `+ originalAmountPaise` | None | Increases shop debt |
| **Posted Supplier Bill** | **+ (Positive)** | `+ totalPaise` | None | Increases shop debt |
| **Accepted Credit Note** | **- (Negative)** | `- acceptedCreditPaise` | None | Decreases shop debt |
| **Outgoing Supplier Payment** | **- (Negative)** | `- totalAmountPaise` | `Out` (signed `-qty`) | Decreases shop debt |
| **Incoming Supplier Refund** | **+ (Positive)** | `+ amountPaise` | `In` (signed `+qty`) | Increases shop debt / reduces credit |
| **Payment Reversal** | **+ (Positive)** | `+ amountPaise` | `In` (signed `+qty`) | Offsets payment; restores debt |
| **Credit Note Reversal** | **+ (Positive)** | `+ acceptedCreditPaise` | None | Offsets credit note; restores debt |
| **Refund Reversal** | **- (Negative)** | `- amountPaise` | `Out` (signed `-qty`) | Offsets refund; restores credit |
| **Document Allocation** | **0 (Neutral)** | Document balance shift | None | **0** (Net position unchanged) |
| **Allocation Reversal** | **0 (Neutral)** | Document balance shift | None | **0** (Net position unchanged) |

### Net Position Equation
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Net Position} = & +\sum \text{OpeningPayables.originalAmountPaise} \\
& +\sum \text{PostedBills.totalPaise} \\
& -\sum \text{SupplierCreditNotes.acceptedCreditPaise} \\
& -\sum \text{SupplierPayments.totalAmountPaise} \\
& +\sum \text{SupplierRefunds.amountPaise} \\
& +\sum \text{PaymentReversals.amountPaise} \\
& +\sum \text{CreditNoteReversals.amountPaise} \\
& -\sum \text{RefundReversals.amountPaise}
\end{aligned}$$

### Reconciled Statement Breakdown
$$\text{Net Position} \equiv (\text{Gross Outstanding Payables}) - (\text{Available Unallocated Credits})$$
Where:
- $\text{Gross Outstanding Payables} = \sum \text{purchases.duePaise} + \sum \text{openingPayables.remainingAmountPaise}$
- $\text{Available Unallocated Credits} = \sum \text{supplierAdvances.remainingAmountPaise}$

### Safe Account Projection Initialization & Protection
Normal financial services **never** call a blind reconciliation routine that overwrites live balances.
1. **Migration Marker:** Company setup checks `companySettings.phase3Migration.status`.
2. **One-Time Idempotent Initialization:**
   - Scans historical `accountMovements` for the tenant.
   - Calculates initial balances: $\text{Cash} = \sum (\text{qty})$, $\text{Bank} = \sum (\text{qty})$ using signed integers.
   - Upserts `tenantAccountBalances` with `version: 1`.
   - Sets `phase3Migration.status = 'Completed'`.
   - Repeated calls immediately return the existing completed status.
   - If `tenantAccountBalances.version > 1` (live Phase 3 postings already exist), initialization **cannot** overwrite them.
3. **Runtime Posting Guard:** All financial endpoints assert `phase3Migration.status === 'Completed'`.
4. **Read-Only Invariant Check:** A separate maintenance endpoint `GET /api/purchases/reconciliation/accounts` compares balances without mutating data.

### Concurrent Idempotency Execution
1. Unique index on `{ tenantId: 1, idempotencyKey: 1 }`.
2. Concurrent requests with the same key cause an atomic unique index conflict on insert.
3. Caught and handled by returning HTTP 409: `"Concurrent request with same idempotency key in progress. Please retry shortly."`
4. Failed transactions are **never** persisted as successful cached responses.

## 10. Four-Tier Stock Availability & Condition Tracking

### Condition Buckets
- **Physical On-Hand:** $\sum (\text{quantitySellable} + \text{quantityReserved} + \text{quantityDefective})$ across `stockLots`.
- **Sellable On-Hand:** $\sum \text{quantitySellable}$.
- **Available to Sell:** $\text{Sellable On-Hand} - \text{quantityReserved}$.
- **Defective Units:** $\sum \text{quantityDefective}$. Quarantined units cannot be sold, but are eligible for supplier returns.

### Serialization Reconciliation Invariant
For every serialized product lot:
$$\text{count}(\text{serialUnits.status === 'InStock'}) \equiv \text{stockLots.quantitySellable}$$
$$\text{count}(\text{serialUnits.status === 'Defective'}) \equiv \text{stockLots.quantityDefective}$$
$$\text{count}(\text{serialUnits.status === 'Returned'}) \equiv \text{stockLots.quantityReturned}$$
