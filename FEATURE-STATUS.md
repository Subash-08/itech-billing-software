Two-source lot returns, invoice-bound serial returns, unpaid due-first offset, warranty replacement lineage, and batch PDF exports verified.
- **Phase 5 (Cash & Account, Daily Closing, Service Jobs, Enquiries, WhatsApp, Dues):** Live and fully verified:
  - **Cash & Account Register (`/register`):** Connected to authoritative MongoDB `/api/money` endpoints with real-time balance calculations, date/account/category filtering, and atomic transfer/reversal flows.
  - **Auth & Profit Unlock:** Session-bound profit unlock with server rate limiting and 10-minute expiry protection. Demo passwords removed.
  - **Daily Closing & Holidays (`/profit`):** Connected to live `/api/closings`, `/api/closings/[date]`, `/api/closings/[date]/profit`, and `/api/closings/[date]/draft` routes. Unified business day gate `DAY-${tenantId}` with optimistic reviewVersion checking; ExpenseReversal offset ensures operating expenses reflect net ledger state; manual profit entry only; unclosed invoice profit invalidated on return; chronological closing strictly enforced.
  - **Service Jobs (`/services`):** Connected to live `/api/services` endpoints. Consumed parts stock conservation via `quantityConsumed` bucket and `ConsumedInService` serial status; service invoicing with `ConsumedPart` lines without double stock decrement; full customer return lifecycle restores `quantityConsumed` to `quantitySellable`, transitions serials back to `InStock`, and marks warranties `Returned`.
  - **Enquiries (`/enquiries`):** Connected to `/api/enquiries` with monotonic `ENQ-YYYY-XXXX` sequencing, customer snapshotting, and rule preventing manual `Won` status without an issued invoice.
  - **Dues & Reminders (`/dues`):** Live invoices and purchases dues calculation with persistent `promisedPaymentDate` via `PATCH /api/sales/invoices/[id]/due-date` and `PATCH /api/purchases/[id]/due-date` with audit notes.
  - **WhatsApp & Communication (`/communication`):** Single-customer WhatsApp message preview with E.164 phone normalization and direct `https://wa.me/...` launch in new tab.
  - **Document Library, Storage & Templates (`/documents`, `/templates`):** Connected to live issued invoices, template engine, and `POST /api/files` / `GET /api/files`. Private filesystem storage with root `PRIVATE_STORAGE_ROOT=D:/AI/itech-private-dev`, read-only storage status card in Settings, file orphan cleanup preserving company logos, service photos, template logos, and invoice snapshots. Full ZIP PDF export renders active/frozen template layouts, fonts, colors, and dynamic columns.
  - **Dashboard & Reports (`/`, `/reports`):** Connected to `/api/company/dashboard` and `/api/company/reports` computing live KPIs, sales, collections, cash/bank drawer balances, active service jobs, dues, and tax summaries.
